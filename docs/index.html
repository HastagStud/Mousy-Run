MOUSY RUN

Draw a ramp. Climb as high as you can. Green is safe, amber is a gamble — and don't let the cats get you.

PLAY

Opens the game in a new tab. Free to play, no download, no sign-up. Works with a finger or a mouse.

How to Play

  • 1Draw a ramp. Press and drag from the mouse to draw a ramp, then let go and he climbs it.
  • 2Green is safe, amber is a gamble. A green ramp passed every check. An amber ramp might still work, but something is off — a trap, a cat, or a landing that isn't quite there.
  • 3One line at a time. A new draw replaces the ramp you have, so draw the next one while he's climbing to keep chaining upward.
  • 4Watch out for traps and cats. Touching one ends the run. Fall, and once the ground has scrolled away, the run is over. Your score is the highest row you reach.
