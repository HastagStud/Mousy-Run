What we collect

Mousy Run does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or give us your name, email address, or any other personal information to play. Your best score is saved only on your own device, in your browser's local storage, and is never sent to us.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to understand how the site is used — for example, how many people visit and which pages they view. Google Analytics collects standard, anonymized usage data such as the pages you visit, the type of device and browser you use, and approximate location derived from your IP address. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. You can also block cookies in your browser settings; the game will still work.

Children

Mousy Run is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
